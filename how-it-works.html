🌾 The Farmer Journey

From sign-up to your first advisory, step by step

No sales calls, no onboarding calls. Here's exactly what happens when you join FarmIQ.

1

Sign up with just an email

Name, email, password. No phone number required. This unlocks location-based weather, crop advisory, and the Farm Assistant.

2

Select your crop

Choose from Corn, Soybeans, Wheat, Vegetables, or Grapes. Advisory logic is meaningfully different per crop — grape heat/irrigation sensitivity is not treated the same as wheat harvest timing.

3

Enable location

One-tap browser geolocation, or enter your location manually if you'd rather not share it — either way you get a real, local forecast. We never sell or share your location data with third parties.

4

Get your 15-day forecast

Temperature highs/lows, rainfall, rain probability, and wind — pulled live and rendered for outdoor readability.

5

Get your daily advisory

Plain-language, crop-specific recommendations generated from your forecast — irrigation timing, heat stress flags, disease pressure, harvest windows.

6

Ask Farm Assistant

Follow-up questions get answered in context — your crop, your weather, your location — through a simple chat interface.
