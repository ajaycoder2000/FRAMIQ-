🔒 Privacy

Privacy Policy

Plain language, because you should be able to tell what happens to your data without a lawyer.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

The short version

We collect the minimum needed to show you a forecast and advisory: your name and email so you can sign in, and a location so we know which field to forecast. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't share your farm data with agribusiness, input suppliers, or anyone else.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name and email address, handled by our authentication provider. We never ask for a phone number.
  • Location — either coordinates from your browser's location permission, or coordinates you type in manually. Used only to request a forecast for that point.
  • Farm preferences — your selected crop, unit preference and language, stored in your own browser.
  • Assistant questions — the text of questions you send to Farm Assistant, plus your crop, location and current forecast as context for the answer.

We do not collect field boundaries, yield data, machinery data, soil tests, or financial records.

Where your data actually goes

We use a small number of processors, each for one job:

  • Clerk — authentication. Holds your name, email and login session.
  • Open-Meteo — weather data. Receives the coordinates being forecast, with no account information attached.
  • Google (Gemini API) — powers Farm Assistant. Receives your question plus crop, location label and forecast context. Your name and email are never sent.
  • Vercel — hosting. Processes standard server request logs.

That's the full list. No advertising networks, no data brokers, no analytics profiling.

Stored in your browser, not on our servers

Your location, selected crop, unit preference and language choice are kept in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device, and clearing your browser data removes them. We don't hold a server-side copy of your field location.

Your rights (GDPR and equivalents)

If you're in the EU, UK, or another region with comparable law, you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

In practice: browser-stored data is yours to clear at any time, and for account data, email support@farmiq.app and we'll action it within 30 days.

Location permission

Browser location is always opt-in — your browser asks, and declining is fine. Manual coordinate entry gets you the same forecast without granting the permission.

Retention

Account data is kept while your account is open and deleted after closure. Assistant questions are sent to Google for the purpose of generating your answer and are not stored in a FarmIQ database. Server logs are short-lived and rotate automatically.

Contact

Questions about any of this: support@farmiq.app. If we change this policy materially, we'll update the date above and tell account holders.
