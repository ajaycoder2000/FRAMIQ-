⚠️ The most important part: advisory is guidance, not a guarantee

FarmIQ gives you weather forecasts and crop advisory to inform your decisions. It does not make them for you, and it cannot see your field.

Weather forecasts are inherently uncertain, and that uncertainty grows across a 15-day window. Advisory is generated from that forecast plus general agronomic rules — it doesn't know your soil, your crop's exact growth stage, your local pest pressure, or your equipment.

Every planting, irrigation, spraying and harvest decision remains yours. For decisions with serious financial or safety consequences, confirm with your own observations, your agronomist, or your local extension service. FarmIQ is not liable for crop loss, yield shortfall, or other damages arising from decisions made using the service.

What the service is

FarmIQ provides 15-day weather forecasts for a location you choose, crop-specific daily advisory for the crops we support, and a Farm Assistant that answers questions using your crop, location and forecast as context. Features may change as the product develops.

Farm Assistant and AI-generated answers

Farm Assistant is powered by a third-party AI model. Like any AI, it can be confidently wrong. It will not give you pesticide or fertiliser application rates, product recommendations, or legal spray windows — those are regulated and vary by jurisdiction, so check the product label and your local rules. Treat any diagnosis it offers as a hypothesis to verify in the field, not a conclusion.
