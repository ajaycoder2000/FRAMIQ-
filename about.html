🌱 About FarmIQ

Built for the farmer left out of the enterprise bundle

This page is for growers who want the full picture, and for investors evaluating the company. Farmer-facing product stays the focus — this is the supporting detail.

Core Pitch

Small and mid-size farms, specialty growers, and vineyards are priced out of or underserved by large bundled ag-tech platforms — Climate FieldView, John Deere Ops Center, Xarvio — which are built around equipment ecosystems, dealer relationships, or enterprise contracts. FarmIQ answers only to the farmer paying for it: a focused weather and advisory product, priced low, sold direct.

Technical Feasibility & Stack

The MVP is a static, multi-page frontend (HTML/CSS/vanilla JS) using Open-Meteo for forecast data and a local rule-based advisory/assistant engine — no backend required to demonstrate the core product loop. Production would move auth to Firebase/Supabase, add a lightweight backend for the Farm Assistant (proxying an LLM API so keys never touch the browser), and layer in a proper crop-advisory rules service or model.

Go-to-Market Strategy

Farmer-to-farmer channels only, deliberately skipping co-op and distributor partnerships that would reintroduce middleman economics:

  • Reddit farming communities (r/farming, r/vineyard, r/agriculture)
  • Regional Facebook farming groups
  • Ag YouTube and TikTok creators
  • Regional farm shows and field days

Alternative Monetization

Freemium free tier, a low flat monthly subscription, and a seasonal/annual option for growers who'd rather pay once per growing season. No enterprise or per-seat pricing — kept intentionally simple.

MVP Roadmap

  • Static MVP: multi-page site, local auth, Open-Meteo forecast, rule-based advisory (this build)
  • Backend auth (Firebase/Supabase) + persistent user profiles
  • Farm Assistant backed by a real LLM via a secure backend endpoint
  • Push/SMS alerts for high-risk advisory events
  • Expanded crop library and regional forecast tuning
